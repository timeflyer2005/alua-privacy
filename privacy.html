Alua. Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Contact: jerryyan20052015@gmail.com

Alua is a training and recovery journal for athletes. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app handles and what happens to it. The short version: your data is used to run the app for you — it is never sold, never shared for advertising, and never used to track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect and why

  • Account information. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address and a unique account identifier. This exists so your data belongs to you and only you, and so it can be restored when you sign in on a new device.
  • Health and fitness data from WHOOP. If you connect a WHOOP account, Alua retrieves your recovery scores, sleep data, heart-rate metrics, and workout data through WHOOP's official API, with your explicit authorization. This data powers every screen of the app. You can disconnect WHOOP at any time in Settings, which stops all further access.
  • Content you create. Journal entries, session reflections, and soreness check-ins are stored on your device and backed up to your account so they survive a lost or new phone. Backups are stored encrypted at rest.
  • Push notification token. If you enable notifications, we store the device token Apple issues so the app can notify you when a workout is scored.

AI-generated insights

Alua's Insights feature sends a compact summary of your recent metrics, journal excerpts, and soreness check-ins to Anthropic's Claude API to generate your weekly and monthly coaching summaries. This data is used only to generate your summary. Per Anthropic's API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train their models.

Service providers

Alua runs on a small set of infrastructure providers, each processing data solely to operate the app: Supabase (database and backend hosting), Anthropic (AI summary generation), Apple and Google (sign-in), and WHOOP (the source of your fitness data, under your authorization). We do not use advertising networks or third-party analytics.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data.
  • We never share your data for advertising or marketing.
  • We never use your data to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Deleting your data

  • Clear data (Settings → Data) permanently deletes everything stored on your phone and in your cloud backup.
  • Delete account (Settings → Account) permanently deletes your account and every piece of data associated with it, including your WHOOP connection and backups.
  • Disconnect WHOOP (Settings) deletes the stored WHOOP tokens and stops all data access.

Children

Alua is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
