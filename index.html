Alua

A training and recovery journal for athletes who wear a WHOOP.

Your sessions arrive on their own, already filled in with strain, heart-rate zones and calories. Your only job is the part no sensor can capture — how it actually felt. Then a coach reads both together and tells you something you didn't already know.

How Alua uses WHOOP data

The member connects their own account. Alua never holds WHOOP credentials. Members sign in through WHOOP's official OAuth flow and grant consent; the app then reads only that member's own data through the public WHOOP API.

What it reads. Cycles, recovery, sleep and workouts — day strain, recovery score, HRV, resting heart rate, sleep stages and sleep need, plus each workout's strain, duration, heart-rate zones and calories.

What Alua adds. A written or dictated reflection on each session, and a body map where the athlete taps muscles to rate soreness from 1 to 5 — the context the numbers alone don't carry.

What the athlete gets back. Daily, weekly and monthly summaries written from their real numbers alongside their own words: a run of short nights tied to a drop in recovery, or a shoulder rated 4/5 across two consecutive sessions.

Nothing is scraped, sold, or used for advertising. Alua is free, with no subscription and no in-app purchase, and displays no WHOOP branding or imagery.
