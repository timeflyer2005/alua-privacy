LaneIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

LaneIQ ("we", "us", "the app") is a training companion for swimmers that connects to your WHOOP account and lets you record how you feel before and after practice. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We take your health data seriously and only use it to run the features you ask for.

Information we collect

  • WHOOP data. With your permission, and only after you connect your WHOOP account, we access physiological data from WHOOP such as recovery score, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, strain, and workout details.
  • Your entries. The check-ins and journal notes you write in the app — energy, mood, soreness, what you want to work on, and any custom metrics you add.
  • Profile information. The account details you provide, such as your name, email, and swimming events.

How we use your information

We use your information only to provide the app's features: displaying your metrics, saving your journal entries, painting your recovery calendar, and generating your weekly and monthly AI summaries. We do not use your data for advertising.

How your data is stored

Your data is stored in our backend provider, Supabase, and is protected so that each user can access only their own records. Your WHOOP login credentials are never stored by us; we use WHOOP's secure authorization (OAuth) and keep only the access tokens needed to sync your data on your device.

Third-party services

To provide the app we share data with a small number of service providers, strictly to deliver features you request:

  • WHOOP — source of your physiological data, accessed with your authorization.
  • Supabase — secure database and authentication.
  • An AI provider — used to generate your written summaries. Only the data needed to produce a summary is sent, and it is not used to train third-party models.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Your choices and rights

You can disconnect your WHOOP account at any time from within the app, which stops any further data sync. You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account and all associated data by contacting us at the address below. When you delete your account, we remove your stored entries, profile, and cached WHOOP data.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your data from our systems within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it by law.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections and access controls that limit each user to their own records. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and to promptly address any issues.

Children's privacy

Competitive swimmers may be minors. If you are under 18, please use LaneIQ only with the consent and involvement of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided us data without appropriate consent, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
